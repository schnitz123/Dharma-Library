ཁྱོད་ཐ་དད་ཀྱང་ཡིན། ཁྱོད་ཡིན་པ་མི་སྲིད་པ་ཡང་ཡིན་པའི་གཞི་མཐུན་དུ་དམིགས་པ་དེ། རང་ཉིད་འགལ་བ་ཡིན་པའི་མཚན་ཉིད།
**Definition of being mutually exclusive with x:** That observed as the common locus of
1. being different from x; and
2. it being x is impossible.

e.g. existence and non-existence, pot and pillar, hot and cold

**When divided:**
1. [[Preliminaries/Collected Topics/mutually negating contradiction\|mutually negating contradiction]] ཕན་ཚུན་སྤང་འགལ།
2. [[Preliminaries/Collected Topics/non-simultaneous abiding contradiction\|non-simultaneous abiding contradiction]] ལྷན་ཅིག་མི་གནས་འགལ།

ཁྱོད་དེ་དང་ཐ་དད་གང་ཞིག དེ་ལྡོག་སྟབས་ཀྱིས་ཁྱོད་ལྡོག་དགོས། འབྲེལ་བའི་མཚན་ཉིད།
**Definition of being related with x:**
{ #35857a}

1. It is different from x; and
2. By eliminating it, x is necessarily eliminated.

**When relation is divided:** 
1. [[Preliminaries/Collected Topics/Related as same nature\|Related as same nature]] བདག་གཅིག་འབྲེལ།
2. [[Preliminaries/Collected Topics/Causal relationship\|Causal relationship]] དེ་བྱུང་འབྲེལ།

Otherwise (not related but different): unrelated different objects འབྲེལ་མེད་དོན་གཞན།

---
There are four possibilities between contradiction and relation:
1. Both: fire and smoke
2. Contradiction: pot and pillar
3. Relation: product and impermanent
4. Neither: pot and pot / male and human
